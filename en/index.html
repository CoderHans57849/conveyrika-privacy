BaitGS · Conveyrika

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. App and contact

This policy applies to the Android game Conveyrika (Russian title: «Конвейрика: грузы по местам»), application ID com.baitgs.conveyrika. The developer publishes the game under the name BaitGS. Contact for privacy and support: coderhans@yandex.ru.

The game has no registration, user accounts, chat, in-app purchases or developer-operated player profile server.

2. Data on your device

Completed levels, stars, unlocked orders and decorations, the last selected level, music and effects volume, and language preference are stored on your device. The game code does not upload these saves to the developer's server. Backups made by Android, an app store or your device manufacturer are subject to the relevant service's settings and policies.

You can delete local data using Android's app data controls. This also removes game progress. Uninstalling removes local app data, but does not necessarily remove platform backups.

3. Advertising and third-party services

The game integrates Yandex Mobile Ads SDK for a banner at the bottom of the gameplay screen and interstitial ads after tapping Next. The advertising integration includes AppMetrica components for advertising measurement. The developer does not add separate gameplay analytics events.

Depending on the platform, settings, identifier availability and applicable restrictions, Yandex services may process technical device, operating system and app information, network information including IP address, device or advertising identifiers, and information about impressions, clicks and technical errors. Purposes include delivering and measuring ads, advertising statistics, abuse prevention and SDK operation. This information may be sent to Yandex servers and processed by its service providers under its documents.

The current app passes a no-consent flag to the SDK for processing that requires consent, disables SDK location tracking, and enables age-restricted audience mode. These settings do not mean that all technical network requests cease. The game does not request precise location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

See Yandex's privacy policy, the AppMetrica data processing terms and advertising SDK privacy settings. Processing that requires consent under applicable law must have an appropriate legal basis; merely using the game does not replace such consent.

4. Support requests

When you email the developer, they receive your sender address and the information you voluntarily include. This is used to respond, troubleshoot and handle data requests. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. Correspondence is also processed by Yandex's email service.

5. Retention, security and disclosure

Local progress remains until you or the system delete it. Correspondence is retained as needed to resolve your request, handle disputes and meet applicable obligations; you may request deletion using the contact email. Yandex determines advertising service retention periods and processing locations subject to applicable law. Third-party services may involve transfers outside your country.

The developer does not sell user saves or correspondence. Access to support requests is limited to support needs and legal requirements. No device or network transmission can be guaranteed absolutely secure.

6. Your choices and children

You can clear app data, restrict network access through your device, adjust advertising identifier availability in Android settings, and ask for access, correction or deletion of information you have sent the developer. Unavailable ads should not prevent level progression. For information processed by Yandex, use the contacts and mechanisms in its policy. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to object, restrict processing or contact a supervisory authority.

The game does not ask children to create accounts or provide personal details. Age-restricted audience mode is enabled in the ad integration. Parents or guardians can report information inadvertently submitted by a child to coderhans@yandex.ru.

7. Links and this page

Privacy, support and advertising links may open external apps or sites with their own policies. This page is hosted by GitHub Pages. No advertising scripts, analytics counters or forms have been added to it; GitHub may process technical request information under its privacy statement.

8. Updates

Changes to features or data practices will be reflected here with a new date. Updating this text alone does not replace any notice or consent required for material changes.
